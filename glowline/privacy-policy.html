Glowline Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 November 2026 · Terms of Service

This policy explains how Scool1965 Studios ("we") handles information in the Glowline mobile game ("the game"). It applies on every app store where the game is offered.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress, coins, stars, boosters, settings, purchase records and a random Player Support ID are saved in encrypted form on your device only. We do not run game servers and you do not create an account. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location. Uninstalling the game deletes this data.

Advertising

When your device is online, the game shows ads served by Start.io (Start.io Inc.) and, where enabled, Google AdMob. These ad networks and their partners may collect and use device information such as your advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, device and app details, and how you interact with ads, to deliver, measure and, where you allow it, personalise ads, and to prevent fraud. Read the Start.io Privacy Policy, the Google Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Where consent is required (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), ads are shown according to your consent choices. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, at any time in your device settings.

The game works fully offline. When there is no connection, no ads are requested and nothing is sent.

In-app purchases

Optional purchases are processed entirely by Google Play (or the app store you installed from) under that store's terms and privacy policy. We never receive or store your card number, bank details, name or billing address. The store gives the game an order ID and purchase token so it can deliver your item once, restore one-time purchases on a new install, and prevent duplicate deliveries. These are kept on your device with your saved game.

Data collected and shared (summary)

  • Collected by Start.io and Google AdMob (third parties): advertising ID and other device identifiers, approximate location derived from IP address, interactions with ads, and diagnostics. Used for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Encrypted in transit.
  • Collected by Google Play Billing (third party): purchase history for purchases you make. Used to process payments.
  • Collected by us: nothing. We do not operate servers and no gameplay data leaves your device.
  • Sold: we do not sell your data.

Children

Glowline is a general-audience game and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights and data deletion

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA or India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data, to object to or restrict its use, or to withdraw consent. Because game data stays on your device, you can delete all of it at any time by uninstalling the game or clearing its storage in Android Settings > Apps > Glowline > Storage > Clear storage. To delete advertising data, reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings, use Google My Ad Center, or contact the ad network directly. Purchase records are held by Google Play and can be managed from your Google account. You can also ask us for help using the contact link below; we aim to respond within 30 days.

Security

Saved game data is encrypted and protected against tampering on your device. Information sent to ad networks travels over encrypted connections.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game changes. The effective date above shows the latest version.

Contact

For questions or requests about this policy, open a support request. If it concerns your saved game, include the Player Support ID shown at the bottom of the game's Settings screen.
