Polarity Pop Privacy Policy

Effective date: 22 November 2026 · Terms of Service

This policy explains how Scool1965 Studios ("we") handles information in the Polarity Pop mobile game ("the game"). It applies to the game on every app store where it is offered.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress, coins, stars, boosters, settings, purchase status and a random Player Support ID are saved in encrypted form on your device only. We do not run game servers and you do not create an account. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location. Uninstalling the game deletes this data.

Advertising

When your device is online, the game shows ads served by Google AdMob. Google and its partners may collect and use device information such as your advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, device and app details, and how you interact with ads, in order to deliver, measure and, where you allow it, personalize ads, and to prevent fraud. Learn more about how Google uses information from apps that use its services and read the Google Privacy Policy.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland or another region where consent is required, the game asks for your choice before requesting ads. You can change it at any time from the game's Settings > Ad Privacy Choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device settings.

The game works fully offline. When there is no connection, no ads are requested and nothing is sent.

In-app purchases

Optional purchases are processed entirely by Google Play (or the app store you installed from) under that store's terms and privacy policy. We never receive or store your card number, bank details, name or billing address. The store gives the game an order ID and a purchase token so the game can deliver your item once, restore one-time purchases such as No Ads on a new install, and prevent duplicate deliveries. These are kept on your device with your saved game.

Data collected and shared (summary)

  • Collected by Google AdMob (third party): advertising ID and other device identifiers, approximate location derived from IP address, interactions with ads, and diagnostics. Used for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Encrypted in transit.
  • Collected by Google Play Billing (third party): purchase history for purchases you make. Used to process payments.
  • Collected by us: nothing. We do not operate servers and no gameplay data leaves your device.
  • Sold: no data is sold.

Children

Polarity Pop is a general-audience game and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights and data deletion

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA or India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data, to object to or restrict its use, or to withdraw consent. Because game data stays on your device, you can delete all of it at any time by uninstalling the game or clearing its storage in Android Settings > Apps > Polarity Pop > Storage > Clear storage. To delete advertising data, reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings, or use Google My Ad Center. Purchase records are held by Google Play and can be managed from your Google account. You can also ask us for help using the contact link below; we aim to respond within 30 days.

Security

Saved game data is encrypted and protected against tampering on your device. Information sent to Google for advertising travels over encrypted connections.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game changes. The effective date above shows the latest version, and significant changes will be noted on this page.

Contact

For questions or requests about this policy, open a support request. If it concerns your saved game, include the Player Support ID shown at the bottom of the game's Settings screen.
